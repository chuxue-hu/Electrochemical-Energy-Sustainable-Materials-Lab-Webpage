Join Our Lab

Openings

We welcome curious, motivated researchers who are excited to advance materials science through collaborative and rigorous research.

Ph.D. Students

We welcome prospective doctoral students with a strong interest in materials science, chemistry, physics, engineering, computation, or related fields.

Applicants should follow the University of Macau graduate admissions process and contact the principal investigator to discuss research fit.

Postdoctoral Researchers

Postdoctoral opportunities are available for researchers who wish to develop an independent research profile in a collaborative laboratory environment.

Relevant research experience and a strong publication record are preferred.

Research Assistants & Students

Undergraduate and research-assistant opportunities may be available for students who want hands-on research experience and mentorship.

Interested applicants are encouraged to get in touch with a brief introduction and their academic background.

How to Apply

Please email the principal investigator with your CV, a short statement of research interests, and relevant academic or research materials. Suitable candidates will be contacted about next steps.

Contact Us

For enquiries about available positions, please contact the principal investigator.
