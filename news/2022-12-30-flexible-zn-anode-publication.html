New Publication in Angewandte Chemie International Edition

We are pleased to announce our publication analyzing dendrite issues for zinc anodes in flexible zinc-based wearable energy-storage devices. The work connects flexible-cell configuration with zinc-anode stability.

Title: Dendrite Issues for Zinc Anodes in a Flexible Cell Configuration for Zinc-Based Wearable Energy-Storage Devices

Journal: Angew Chem Int Ed Engl 2022, 61 (25), e202202780

DOI: 10.1002/anie.202202780
