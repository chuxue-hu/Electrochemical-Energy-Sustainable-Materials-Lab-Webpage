New Publication in Small

We are pleased to announce our publication on interfacial modification for a stable zinc anode using a thin separator. The work clarifies how separator-mediated interface regulation improves zinc-anode cycling.

Title: Mechanistic Study of Interfacial Modification for Stable Zn Anode Based on a Thin Separator

Journal: Small 2022, 18 (20), e2201045

DOI: 10.1002/smll.202201045
