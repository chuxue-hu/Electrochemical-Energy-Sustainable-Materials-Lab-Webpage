New Publication in Nano Letters

We are pleased to announce our publication on zinc powder anodes for rechargeable aqueous zinc-based batteries. The work evaluates zinc powder as a practical architecture for rechargeable aqueous storage.

Title: Zinc Powder Anodes for Rechargeable Aqueous Zinc-Based Batteries

Journal: Nano Lett 2024, 24 (14), 4055–4063

DOI: 10.1021/acs.nanolett.4c00101
