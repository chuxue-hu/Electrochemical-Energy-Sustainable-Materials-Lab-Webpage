New Publication in Journal of Materials Chemistry A

We are pleased to announce our publication on cyclized-polyacrylonitrile-modified carbon-nanofibre interlayers for strong polysulfide trapping in lithium-sulfur batteries. The work develops an interlayer strategy for improved sulfur electrochemistry.

Title: Cyclized-Polyacrylonitrile Modified Carbon Nanofiber Interlayers Enabling Strong Trapping of Polysulfides in Lithium-Sulfur Batteries

Journal: Journal of Materials Chemistry A 2016, 4 (33), 12973–12980

DOI: 10.1039/C6TA03918A
