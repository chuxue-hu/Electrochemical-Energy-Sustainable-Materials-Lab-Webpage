New Publication in Joule

We are pleased to announce our publication examining hidden soft shorts in zinc-metal-anode research. The article emphasizes the importance of identifying subtle short-circuit pathways when evaluating aqueous zinc batteries.

Title: “Soft Shorts” Hidden in Zinc Metal Anode Research

Journal: Joule 2022, 6 (2), 273–279

DOI: 10.1016/j.joule.2021.12.009
