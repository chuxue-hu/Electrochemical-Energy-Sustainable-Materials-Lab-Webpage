New Publication in Energy Materials and Devices

We are pleased to announce our publication examining the transformative potential of cellulose in energy-storage systems. The work highlights cellulose-based materials as a sustainable platform for energy technologies.

Title: The Transformative Potential of Cellulose in Energy Storage Systems

Journal: Energy Materials and Devices 2025, 3 (4), 9370076

DOI: 10.26599/EMD.2025.9370076
