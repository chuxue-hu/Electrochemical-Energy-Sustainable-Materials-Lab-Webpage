New Publication in Advanced Science

We are pleased to announce our publication on fixed-linkage membranes for ultra-stable ion transport in long-life alkaline flow batteries. The work connects membrane architecture with durable transport and cycling behavior.

Title: Fixed-Linkage Enabled Ultra-Stable Ion Transport Channels in Membranes for Long-Life Alkaline Flow Batteries

Journal: Advanced Science 2026, 13 (33), e24374

DOI: 10.1002/advs.202524374
